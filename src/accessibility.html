Accessibility

I want everyone to be able to read this site. Here is what I have done and where it still falls short. Last updated October 2026.

What is in place

Every page is built with proper headings, links, and buttons, so screen readers and keyboard navigation can move through it. You can reach every link, button, and form on the site with the Tab key, and the item you are on is outlined so you can see where you are. Photos have descriptions for screen readers, and the signup boxes are labeled. The site works on phones, tablets, and desktops, and text is set in plain, high-contrast white on black or black on off-white. If your device is set to reduce motion, the site's animations are turned down.

Where it still falls short

Some older episodes and poems were moved over from Substack, and a few of their photos may not have full descriptions yet. I am adding them as I go. The heart that follows your mouse on desktop is decoration only; it does not appear on phones or touch screens. I have not had the whole site tested by an outside accessibility reviewer, so there may be problems I have not found.

Tell me about a problem

If something on this site is hard for you to read or use, message me through any of the links in the footer below. Tell me the page and what went wrong, and I will fix it or find another way to get you what you need. You can also read every episode and poem by email through my Substack.
