Where Should I Mail Your Poem and Letter?

Thank you for becoming a paid member. On the last day of every month I write you your own poem and letter by hand and mail it to your door, anywhere in the world. If you join by the 15th, your first one is mailed at the end of that same month, and if you join after the 15th, it’s mailed at the end of the next month.

Only I can see your address. I use it to address your envelope and for nothing else, and I delete it if you cancel or ask me to. Moved? Fill this out again with your new address and I’ll use the newest one.

If you would like me to delete your information permanently at any time, please email me at sheridan@whatsheridansaid.com.

Thank you, it’s on its way to my list.

Your first poem and letter will be mailed on the last day of the month.
