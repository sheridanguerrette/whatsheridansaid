The Fine Print

Privacy Policy

What I collect, why, and who sees it. Last updated October 2026.

Your email address

When you type your email into a subscribe box on this site, I save it so I can add you to my newsletter list on Substack. I use it to send you What Sheridan Said and nothing else. I never sell it, rent it, or share it. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of any email, or ask me to delete it.

Paid members and mailing addresses

Paid members receive a handwritten poem and letter in the mail each month. To send it, I ask for a mailing address through a private form. Only I can see those addresses. I use them to address your envelope and for no other reason, and I delete an address when you cancel or ask me to.

Payments

Paid memberships are handled by Substack and its payment processor. I never see or store your card details.

Cookies and analytics

This site doesn't use advertising trackers. Hosting and fonts are provided by third parties (Netlify and Google Fonts), which may log standard technical information such as your IP address when your browser loads a page.

Your choices

To see, correct, or delete anything I hold about you, message me through any of the links in the footer below and I'll take care of it.
